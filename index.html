What I build

Voice agents, automations and full-stack apps that answer, qualify and book leads, so the business runs without copy-paste.

  • AI voice agents

    Inbound receptionists and outbound callers on VAPI and Retell, connected to Twilio, n8n and your calendar. They pick up every call, qualify the lead and put the appointment in the diary.

  • AI agents and chatbots

    Chat and email agents that answer from your own knowledge base (Supabase, RAG), draft replies for your team and hand off to a person when it matters.

  • n8n and workflow automation

    n8n, Make.com and Zapier workflows that carry leads and data between your apps, from form fill to follow-up SMS, with no copy-pasting in between.

  • GoHighLevel and CRM setup

    GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho and Pipedrive set up to work for you: pipelines, lead routing, follow-up sequences and API integrations.

  • Claude Code apps

    Full-stack apps built with Claude Code: command-center dashboards, custom CRMs and landing pages on React, Next.js and Supabase.

  • Data and dashboards

    Scrapers, pipelines and dashboards in Python, SQL, Power BI and Looker Studio that turn raw data into numbers you can act on.
